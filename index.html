International Lymphoma Radiation Oncology Group

About Us

Welcome! The mission of the International Lymphoma Radiation Oncology Group (ILROG) is to improve the outcomes for patients with hematologic malignancies through the appropriate integration of high-quality radiation therapy in the management of lymphoma.

It is our hope that with this new website, it will be easier to engage with the lymphoma physician and patient communities and to disseminate existing and new evidence. Together, we can ensure that all lymphoma patients receive the evidence-based, multidisciplinary care they deserve.

ILROG NEXT

A transformation initiative of the International Lymphoma Radiation Oncology Group, bringing together leaders across the world to shape the next generation of guidance, evidence, and collaboration.

  • Create clear pathways for ILROG to engage early and mid-career faculty
  • Convene contemporary topic-focused working groups to advance priority questions
  • Foster cross-committee collaboration so ideas and evidence move between groups
  • Offer opportunities for mentorship, leadership and growth
