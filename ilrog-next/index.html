ILROG NEXT

Shaping the next chapter of lymphoma radiation oncology

A transformation initiative of the International Lymphoma Radiation Oncology Group, bringing together leaders across the world to shape the next generation of guidance, evidence, and collaboration.

Mission

  • Create clear pathways for ILROG to engage early and mid-career faculty
  • Convene contemporary topic-focused working groups to advance priority questions
  • Foster cross-committee collaboration so ideas and evidence move between groups
  • Offer opportunities for mentorship, leadership and growth
  • Create a professional community of hematologic-focused radiation oncologists
  • Critically revisit how academic and clinical information is generated and disseminated for maximal reach

ILROG-NEXT is a separate but related effort. It draws leadership and participation from every tier of ILROG and does not replace the existing structure.

Leadership

A steering committee of senior ILROG leaders guides the initiative. The co-leaders run it day to day and connect the steering committee with each working group.

Get involved

Inclusion is a guiding principle of ILROG-NEXT. Working group leaders invite the members they need, and new volunteers are welcome, including trainees and early-career faculty.

All ILROG-NEXT participants should be at least General Members of ILROG.
