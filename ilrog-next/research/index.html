Conditioning radiotherapy before hematopoietic cell transplant

Survey open

Patterns of Practice in Total Body Irradiation and Intensity-Modulated Marrow Irradiation (IMRT-TBI, TMI and TMLI)

Intensity-modulated delivery has made it possible to spare organs beyond the lungs during total body irradiation, and at some point a heavily sculpted plan is no longer TBI but total marrow (TMI) or total marrow and lymphoid irradiation (TMLI). There is no agreed definition of where that line falls. This survey of radiation oncology programs asks which conditioning techniques each institution offers for myeloablative and non-myeloablative regimens, the dose and fractionation schedules used, the delivery platform, and how centers choose between conventional and intensity-modulated TBI. Programs that perform IMRT-TBI are asked which organs they routinely spare, the constraints they apply and the target coverage they accept. A final section asks where respondents would draw the boundary between TBI and TMI/TMLI. The survey takes about 10 to 15 minutes and asks for one response per institution, reflecting institutional practice.
